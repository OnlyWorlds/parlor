# The walks

Browser tests ("walks") that drive the parlor against the live OnlyWorlds server, using test accounts the OnlyWorlds team keeps (named `crew-walk-…`; their logins live in a file outside this repository, named by `CREW_WALK_ACCOUNTS`, and are never printed). Without those accounts you cannot run them; `npm test` is the check. The notes below are for the team.

```bash
export CREW_WALK_ACCOUNTS=<the accounts file>
export WALK_STATE=<a file outside the repo>
node walk/seed.mjs crew-walk-b     # the test world, its charter, fresh invite links (deletes nothing)
node walk/serve.mjs dist 30338     # the built app under its own _headers
PARLOR_URL=http://localhost:30338/ node walk/walk.mjs chromium   # a host, a member, a reader without a PIN
PARLOR_URL=http://localhost:30338/ node walk/walk.mjs firefox
node walk/teardown.mjs             # takes back only what the state recorded, and reads it back
```

The test world is "Parlor ground (crew test)". Its Charter is marked, so it is a team world: its room holds every sitting's lines, so a walk's words carry a run stamp, and the host presses Run a workshop before the host panel's controls (and ends the workshop at the end). A page says its room role on `.top[data-room-role]`, which the walks wait on. The walk plants decoys (a world's own elements in the room's words, without the room mark) and checks they never show as the room. Every key, token and decoy it makes is recorded in `WALK_STATE`, and teardown touches nothing else. A control: point `PARLOR_URL` at an older build.

Sign in with OnlyWorlds has two walks of its own:

- `node walk/signin-oauth.mjs [chromium|firefox]` plays the OnlyWorlds server and serves `https://parlor.onlyworlds.com` from `dist/` under its `_headers` and `_redirects`. No account needed; `DIST=<dir>` runs it on another build as a control.
- `node walk/live-signin-ow.mjs <out-prefix> [engine] [account]` uses the real server and a test account signed in on onlyworlds.com, against `DIST` when set, else the live site (`ROOM=1` comes back into a room). It revokes the token and key the parlor made, by their ids, and a run that throws or rejects still revokes every Parlor token new since its start (`FAIL_AFTER_KEPT=1` forces that failure, to check it).

A work item's address has a played walk of its own, no account needed: `node walk/work-address.mjs [chromium|firefox] [phone]` plays the OnlyWorlds server's worlds, `me`, changes and line-sending doors (every other door answers 404) and serves `https://parlor.onlyworlds.com` from `dist/`, the page signed in with a world key. It opens `#/w/<world>/work/<id>` and reads the Work tab, the card marked and in view, the Answer open and the composer focused (on a phone, the composer's pane), all holding across a feed tick and a reload; another tab, Not now, Answer on another card and the answer sent (a played POST) each give the bar the room's own address, replaced so Back does not return to it and with the same room and a typed draft kept; a slow answer that lands after the tab moved to another work address leaves that address as it is; an id not served says the work item is not here, and says nothing while the first read fails; a seat that may only read is told so on the card, with no composer. `DIST=<dir>` runs it on another build as a control. `signin-oauth.mjs` checks that the address comes back whole through Sign in with OnlyWorlds.

An element's card has a played walk of its own, no account needed: `node walk/element-card.mjs [chromium|firefox] [phone]` plays the same doors as the work item's address walk and serves `https://parlor.onlyworlds.com` from `dist/`. It opens a card from Changes (on a phone, a sheet over the panel), reads its words folded under Show all and opened, a long name and a row of wide capitals in the link picture each ending in an ellipsis inside it (fitted by drawn width), the whole name as its accessible name and title, a feed tick that rebuilds the card while focus is on a neighbour (focus and the fold stay), a step to a neighbour and back by Tab and Enter (the heading takes focus, the fold closes), Escape in the composer (the card stays), on the card and on the name that opened it (it closes, focus on that name), a known-of stub's card with its mark and words, and a question going to its work item (the Work tab, the item's card focused, its address in the bar). The played changes log also deletes: an element deleted while the person types closes its card and leaves the composer its focus and text; one deleted while focus is in its card puts focus on the panel's Changes tab, never the page. The world is a team world (a marked charter): closing the Rules card gives focus back to Rules, and a card opened from a chip in the room's lines, closed with × (on a phone after the panel's Work tab was chosen), gives focus to the chip (on a phone, to that tab). For a seat that is no keeper a proposed row in Changes is no press; for an owner it opens its card marked proposed, and a hidden neighbour is listed and opens marked. `DIST=<dir>` runs it on another build as a control; `SHOTS=<dir>` keeps a picture at a few steps, and one where a run stops.

A team world's room has its own walk: `node walk/team-world.mjs [chromium|firefox] [phone]` after `seed.mjs crew-walk-b`, against `PARLOR_URL`. The host reads the crew (Team chat, Beasts, People and their AIs, a fold that holds through the feed; a Companion's row opens its room among the beasts and under its person, an Agent's does not), opens the member's room (the member's Note as its status line with its elements; no seat shows as working, though the member holds a taken work item), opens an element's card and steps to a neighbour, writes a line there and reads back its `characters` and the world's mark, replies under it from Team chat and reads back `parent_narrative`, sees Report move the composer, runs a workshop (a line said then carries the sitting's mark; the member sees Specs only while a switch is on), ends it (every switch off, read back), opens Rules and a design's fold. Then it opens crew-walk-b's "Dashboard ground (crew test)" (no Charter mark) read only, every write refused before it leaves, and reads it as a workshop world. One door is played, GET /members on the host's page and the phone's (the test world has no AI seat and no roster Character): the walk's head says what it adds. Everything it makes is recorded, and the world keys it mints by API are revoked by id by `teardown.mjs`.

Work by role has its own walk: `node walk/roles.mjs [chromium|firefox]` after the seed, against `PARLOR_URL`. It makes two Titles (one held by the host), a Character and five work items with the room mark, each recorded so `teardown.mjs` deletes them by id, and reads the Work tab's three groups (for you, for others, addressed to no one). `EXPECT_OLD=1` runs it as the control on a build without the key, where it must fail.

Files on a line have their own walk: `node walk/files.mjs [chromium|firefox]` after the seed, against `PARLOR_URL`. The host attaches a picture and a note, a .gif is refused at the pick, text named .png is refused by OnlyWorlds, a line of files alone has no text, the member sees the files and previews with no Remove, and the host removes one. Every line it makes is recorded so `teardown.mjs` deletes it by id (OnlyWorlds removes a deleted line's files with it). The paste checks run in Chromium only: Firefox's synthetic paste carries no files. `EXPECT_OLD=1` runs it as the control on a build without files, where it must fail.

Reports have their own walk: `node walk/report.mjs [chromium|firefox]` after the seed, against `PARLOR_URL`. A member hands in a free report with a file (Enter makes new lines; files alone are refused as a report), which hangs on the newest sitting by `events` and says so in the room while a workshop runs, and on no sitting otherwise, then a report on a work item that answers it (Ctrl+Enter); the walk reads each element back field by field, unfolds a story and checks it stays open across a redraw, has the host complete the first report in the room (a change to the report after the card opened is refused first, the typed text kept), reads the completed layout on both pages, completes the second (the work item it answers set done, its story and links kept), and measures the composer at 360 px. Then the host's own report: a member sees no keeper part; the host hands in on a second work item with the box ticked, is refused with no short part, then hands in with In short, two lines of What changed and a picked Character, and the walk reads one report born `completed` (its description, its consequences with the line that set the item done, its links) and the item `done`; a second keeper report with nothing under What changed reads `nothing changed`. The work items, the Character and every report are recorded so `teardown.mjs` deletes them by id. `EXPECT_OLD=1` runs it as the control on a build without reports, where it must fail.

Designs have their own walk: `node walk/design.mjs [chromium|firefox]` after the seed, against `PARLOR_URL`. It makes an Object in design, a Construct locked and an Object with no state, reads the Designs tab, answers the Object with a line and reads it back (the part in `objects`, nothing else), sees a rename reach the reply bar and a part that leaves its state clear the reply, then hands in a report on the Object (in `objects`, no `events`). Everything it makes is recorded so `teardown.mjs` deletes it by id. `EXPECT_OLD=1` is the control on a build without Designs, where it must fail.

The room's charter has its own walk: `node walk/charter.mjs [chromium|firefox]` after the seed, against `PARLOR_URL`. It takes the charter profile's mark off the test world's House rules by API, reads that neither page shows it as house rules and only the host is offered to mark it, presses Mark, and reads both pages and the server; whatever happens, it puts the mark back on that Law and reads it back. `EXPECT_OLD=1` is the control on a build that shows an unmarked Charter, where it must fail.

What a guest is served has its own probe, with no app in between: `node --experimental-strip-types walk/guest.mjs` after the seed. The host makes a sitting, a line, a spec, a vote and a board entry in the parlor's own shapes and reads them back; crew-walk-a, lowered from contributor to guest by the owner for the read, reads each by id and by list with a read key (the parlor's way in for a guest), and its role is put back and read back. Everything it makes is recorded for `teardown.mjs`. On 2026-11-11 a guest was served the line (a Message) and none of the sitting, spec, vote or board entry.

A watcher's room has its own walk: `node --experimental-strip-types walk/watcher.mjs [chromium|firefox]` after the seed, against `PARLOR_URL`. The host starts two sittings with a line each; crew-walk-a, lowered to guest for the walk and put back after, opens the room. The test world is a team world, so the watcher must see both sittings' lines, the line saying it can read the room, and the Crew tab showing the crew as OnlyWorlds serves it (a guest's key reads the roster), its own row saying "watcher, you". (A world with no Charter mark shows a watcher the newest sitting's lines only, by their mark; the unit tests hold that.)

A line written by the toolkit's workshop recipe has its own walk: `node --experimental-strip-types walk/recipe.mjs [chromium|firefox]` after the seed, against `PARLOR_URL`. crew-walk-a, a contributor, writes one line exactly as the recipe says (its words in `story`, `x_room_of` the sitting's id) beside a line in the old shape (words in `description`); the host's room must show both lines' words, and crew-walk-a, lowered to guest for one read and put back, finds the line in the current sitting by its mark. `EXPECT_OLD=1` is the control on a build that reads a line's words from `description` only.
